# ATG:BROADCAST Profile

Status: Production candidate

ATG:BROADCAST preserves the persistent campaign, distribution, KOL/influencer activation and attribution functionality previously placed at the root of AGENTROPOLIS-ATG.

It is a bounded domain profile of ATG. It does not redefine the ATG protocol.

## Responsibility split

- **ATG:BROADCAST:** campaign goals, audience selection, KOL matching, channel strategy, distribution, testing, conversion attribution and performance feedback.
- **Utility Grid:** persistent sessions, generation routing, playout, failover, metering, budgets and broadcast receipts.
- **Social Systems:** personas, follows, interactions, communities, audience memory and reputation.
- **ATV Network:** channels, schedules, programming and show packages.
- **BotBae:** persona construction, client operations, routing and supported messaging surfaces.
- **Pixelshop:** offers, products, affiliates and checkout.
- **Chaos Mira:** Web3 worlds and community experiences.
- **BUZZ:** signed collaboration and production events.

## Persistent campaign loop

```text
Campaign mandate
  -> approved Persona Passport
  -> KOL / influencer match
  -> channel and audience plan
  -> time-boxed broadcast lease
  -> ATV / social / game / commerce activation
  -> clips, posts, listicles and live interactions
  -> conversion and community receipts
  -> bounded optimization
  -> human review
  -> renew, revise or stop
```

## Required semantics

Every activation must carry:

- `campaign_id`, `persona_id`, `broadcast_id`, `correlation_id`
- campaign owner and measurable objective
- approved audiences, channels and jurisdictions
- sponsorship, affiliate and AI-disclosure rules
- budget and frequency caps
- content variants and platform permissions
- attribution model and conversion events
- opt-out, takedown and human escalation paths
- provenance, moderation and broadcast receipt references

Audience performance never changes truth-state. High engagement cannot promote an unverified claim, expand an agent's authority, weaken a policy gate, or override persona policy.

## SENSE integration

SENSE dominance and related market/social signals may enter ATG:BROADCAST as evidence-backed signals. A signal can trigger analysis, discussion routing, KOL matching or a proposed campaign mandate, but it does not independently authorize investment, financial execution, promotion, spending, or publication.

## Primary surfaces

Approved programming may route across Social Systems, ATV Network, Pixelshop, Chaos Mira, Terp City, BotBae-supported surfaces, district communities and approved external platforms.

Each consequential action is separately authorized and receipted through the canonical ATG corridor.

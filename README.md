# AGENTROPOLIS ATG

ATG is the go-to-market, distribution, KOL/influencer activation and attribution layer for AGENTROPOLIS.

It does not own generative playout or canonical social identity. ATG consumes the Utility Grid's **Persistent Broadcast Runtime** and Social Systems' persona graph to turn approved agents, KOLs, influencers and hybrid personalities into governed campaigns and persistent media programs.

## Responsibility split

- **ATG:** campaign goals, audience selection, KOL matching, channel strategy, listicles, clips, distribution, testing, conversion attribution and performance feedback.
- **Utility Grid:** persistent sessions, generation routing, playout, failover, metering, budgets and broadcast receipts.
- **Social Systems:** personas, follows, interactions, communities, audience memory and reputation.
- **ATV Network:** channels, schedules, programming and show packages.
- **BotBae:** persona construction and client operations.
- **Pixelshop:** offers, products, affiliates and checkout.
- **Chaos Mira:** Web3 worlds and community experiences.
- **BUZZ:** signed collaboration and production events.

## Persistent campaign loop

```text
Campaign mandate
  -> approved Persona Passport
  -> KOL / influencer match
  -> channel and audience plan
  -> time-boxed broadcast lease
  -> ATV / social / game / commerce activation
  -> clips, posts, listicles and live interactions
  -> conversion and community receipts
  -> bounded optimization
  -> human review
  -> renew, revise or stop
```

## ATG requirements

Every activation must carry:

- `campaign_id`, `persona_id`, `broadcast_id` and `correlation_id`
- campaign owner and measurable objective
- approved audiences, channels and jurisdictions
- sponsorship, affiliate and AI-disclosure rules
- budget and frequency caps
- content variants and platform permissions
- attribution model and conversion events
- opt-out, takedown and human escalation paths
- provenance, moderation and broadcast receipt references

Audience performance never changes truth-state. High engagement cannot promote an unverified claim, expand an agent's authority or override persona policy.

## Primary consumers and surfaces

ATG activates approved programming across Social Systems, ATV Network, Pixelshop, Chaos Mira, Terp City, Discord/BotBae games, district communities and approved external platforms.

The same campaign may produce live programming, short clips, reactions, Spaces, listicles and commerce moments, but each action is separately authorized and receipted.
